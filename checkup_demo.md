## How to read this

- A candidate only means a name looks similar. Many are fans, parodies, unrelated people or unrelated businesses.
- Verify each one by hand before saying anything to anyone. Never claim fraud from this list alone.

## What this check does NOT cover

X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Telegram, marketplaces, app stores, and what AI assistants say about the brand. Those need licensed data or platform partnerships, which is the part a real product provides.

## Before any outreach

A human reviews every finding. One plain-text message to the right role (brand protection, comms, security), evidence inline, no tracking links or attachments, sent from the company's own domain. Check local rules on business email with the company's legal or privacy team.
